Frequently Asked Questions

Is this service free?

Yes, Tamp Mail is completely free to use for everyone.

How long do emails last?

Emails are stored for a limited time on Mail.tm servers, usually until the domain expires or the service clears old data.

Can I send emails?

No, this is a receive-only service to protect against abuse and spam.
