What is Temp Mail?

Temp mail is a disposable email service that allows you to receive emails at a temporary address that self-destructs after a certain time.

How It Works

Simply copy the generated email address, use it for registrations or services, and watch the emails appear instantly in your inbox.

Key Benefits

Keep your real inbox clean, bypass mandatory email verifications, and protect your personal information from data breaches.
